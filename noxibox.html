The NoxiBox is an anti-phone-theft device
  • The NoxiBox is controlled by a pair of ordinary earbuds
  • The NoxiBox can lock your phone into place, making your phone hard to steal
  • When alarmed, the box has an alarm that sounds when the earbuds are not contactable
  • Only your earbuds can deactivate the alarm (Bonded)
  • The NoxiBox can forward media audio from your phone to your earbuds
The NoxiBox is an original invention by Nigel Knox. It was developed by Nigel alone. The project involved..
  • Embeded programming on a MCU
  • Engineering the gears
  • Creating the distinctive opening mechanism
  • Creating a beskpoke circuit board
  • Design and manufacture
